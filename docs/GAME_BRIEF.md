# Game Design Brief: Rockstar

## Concept

Rockstar is a mobile match-3 puzzle game inspired by the accessibility, polish, and long-term progression of popular puzzle games, but with a completely original music-performance theme.

The player completes puzzle levels that feel like live concerts. Every successful match builds crowd excitement. Every power-up feels like a stage effect. Winning a level feels like finishing a successful performance.

## Core gameplay

- Traditional match-3 puzzle mechanics
- Swap adjacent tiles
- Match 3 or more symbols
- Matches clear tiles
- Tiles fall and cascade
- New tiles refill the board
- Larger matches create power-ups
- Power-ups can combine for bigger effects
- Difficulty gradually increases over many levels

## Visual theme

The board represents the performance energy of a concert.

Recommended puzzle symbols:

- Gold record
- Silver record
- Music note
- Star
- Spotlight
- Speaker
- Microphone
- Stage light

Important visual rule:

Puzzle pieces must be simple, iconic, readable, and consistent in shape.
Avoid realistic instruments as board pieces because they are too detailed and irregular at small mobile size.

## Core resource: Crowd Energy

Crowd Energy is the emotional meter of the level.

The player should feel that every good move improves the performance.

Possible feedback:

- crowd cheering
- stage lights becoming brighter
- performer animation
- energy meter filling
- final encore effect on win

## Objectives

Prototype objectives:

- collect 10 gold records
- complete level within 28 moves
- reach required crowd energy

Future objectives:

- clear obstacles
- charge performance meters
- collect specific symbols
- activate stage equipment
- complete concert-related goals

## Power-ups

Music-themed equivalents of traditional boosters:

- Microphone Blast: clears a row or column
- Spotlight Burst: clears a small area
- Stage Explosion: large bomb effect
- Encore Power: combo multiplier
- Superstar Power-Up: clears one selected symbol type

## Progression idea

Long-term career progression could include:

1. School talent contest
2. Garage band
3. Local bar
4. Small club
5. City venue
6. Festival
7. National tour
8. Arena
9. World tour

Important:

The puzzle game must remain the main experience. Career systems support the puzzle loop but do not replace it.
